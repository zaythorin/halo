Used in "Halo Warzone Alpha" as the online map "Silent Cartographer" (replacing the desert map). The model was recentred, its underground facility was removed, its textures were downsized and its geometry was repacked for the game. Ramps, blast doors over the building entrances, water, trees, palms and bushes were added by the game.
